# Product

## The problem

A session ends, and the useful part is still ahead: the evening the anxiety gathers, the night before sleep, the afternoon when a sentence from the room is still around. That material usually leaves as a voice note, a photo in a chat thread, or a promise to “send something.” Clients cannot find it later. Practitioners cannot see what was left with whom.

Connect is the place those materials live.

## Who it is for

**Practitioners** of wellness work that continues outside the hour: somatic therapy, counselling, coaching, meditation teaching, and neighbouring practices. The sample practitioner is Mara Ellison at Stillroom Practice.

**Clients** of those practitioners. They are not browsing a public catalogue. They open a tray their practitioner filled. The sample client view is Jonah Adeyemi. Priya Shah and Luis Ortega are visible on the roster, with their own assignments, so the model is not a single hard-coded person.

Connect is not a marketplace, a public social network, or a clinical record system.

## Jobs

A practitioner can:

- Keep a library of meditations, prompts, lessons, and images.
- See which clients a material was left with.
- Open one client and see that person’s tray.
- Read a short record of recent movement in the practice.
- Speak in a small circle with the people they work with.

A client can:

- See what was left for them, grouped by what to do with it: listen, write, read, look.
- Start a short practice without deciding which one.
- Write into a prompt.
- Leave a note in the circle.

## The sample week

The seed is one coherent practice, not placeholder titles.

- Jonah’s Sundays crowd his chest. He has an eight-minute arrival breath and a window-light still.
- Priya stays near something unfinished. She has a letter she does not send, and a note about keeping that letter in a container.
- Luis is working on the hour before sleep. He has an evening practice, a night still, and a short lesson that refuses to grade him on whether sleep arrives.

Mara’s overview lists the size of the library and a fixed week of movement. The movement is sample copy until there is a database.

## Tone

The interface follows an editorial marketplace: a stone field, black type, a cream page, and a black footer. Titles are a plain grotesque. An italic serif is only for asides. Copy speaks the way Mara would leave a note: specific, short, and without a claim that the exercise will fix the week.

## Out of scope for the product, not only this build

- Emergency or crisis response
- Diagnosis, prescribing, or treatment plans
- A public directory of practitioners
- Replacing the session itself

See [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md). The short version: identity, files, persistence, and realtime delivery are seams with a place to land. They are not pretended into being.
